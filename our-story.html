Our Story

Hey, I’m Jude. I started a pajama company.

When I started picking out my own clothes, I wanted to pick my own pajamas, too. I had my own style. And if I was going to wear something every night, I wanted it to be something I’d actually choose.

That’s why I started Hey Jude. I wanted to make pajamas that feel soft when you put them on and look fun enough that you’ll pull them out of the drawer first.

Preview image

Pajamas by kids, for kids

When I was coming up with my first two sets, I picked flowers for my little sister. For mine, I wanted checker pants and a simple shirt with my logo on it. I like that they’re different. Picking your pajamas is more fun when you get to pick something that feels like you.

I chose cotton because I wanted something soft and breathable. My family helps me with the grown-up parts of running a business, but I get to do one of my favorite parts: picking what the pajamas look like.

Why I say “Better Tomorrows”

In my first pitch, I said tomorrow starts the night before. I still think that’s true. A cozy night is a pretty good way to get ready for whatever you’re doing tomorrow.

I also think every kid deserves something cozy to put on at the end of the day. One of my goals as Hey Jude grows is to help other kids get pajamas of their own. That’s the kind of business I want to build.

Thanks for being here while I get started. Now I want to know: would you pick flowers or checkers?

Jude
Founder, Hey Jude

Soft Sleep. Happy Dreams. Better Tomorrows.
