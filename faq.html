FAQ

Questions grown-ups ask

What are the pajamas made of?
100% cotton. Soft, breathable and comfy all night.
Why should I choose cotton pajamas for my child?
Cotton is soft against the skin and lets air move through the fabric. If your child tends to feel warm in pajamas, cotton is a good option to try for a more comfortable bedtime.
What sizes do you make?
XS · Twinkle (4–5), S · Star (6–7), M · Moon (8–10) and L · Galaxy (11–12). See the Size Guide for measurements.
How do I wash them?
Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. Skip the bleach.
Will they shrink?
Cotton can shrink a little in a hot dryer. Washing cold and drying low keeps them closest to their original size.
Where do you ship?
Within the United States. Details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
Do you deliver locally?
Local delivery is free in much of the Denver metro area. We group deliveries into one weekly window. If your address isn’t eligible, you can still choose shipping. Check your ZIP.
Can I order now?
Yes — pre-orders for our first drop are open. Pick a size, add it to your bag and send a request. You won’t be charged when you submit a request. We’ll email you to confirm what’s in stock, how you’ll receive your order, and your total, along with instructions for paying.
When will my pre-order ship?
We’ll share the timing when we email to confirm your pre-order, and we’ll let you know if anything changes.
