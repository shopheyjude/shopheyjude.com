FAQ

Questions grown-ups ask

What are the pajamas made of?
100% cotton. Soft, breathable and comfy all night.
Why should I choose cotton pajamas for my child?
Cotton is soft against the skin and lets air move through the fabric. If your child tends to feel warm in pajamas, cotton is a good option to try for a more comfortable bedtime.
What sizes do you make?
XS · Twinkle (4–5), S · Star (6–7), M · Moon (8–10) and L · Galaxy (10–12). See the Size Guide for measurements.
Should I size up so they last longer?
Please don’t. Kids’ cotton pajamas are designed to fit snugly — it’s a safety rule for children’s sleepwear — so choose your child’s usual size.
How do I wash them?
Machine wash cold, inside out, with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang to dry. Skip the bleach.
Will they shrink?
Cotton can shrink a little in a hot dryer. Washing cold and drying low keeps them closest to their original size.
Where do you ship?
Within the United States. Details are on our Shipping & Returns page.
Do you deliver locally?
Local delivery is $7 for eligible addresses in ZIP code 80015. We group deliveries into one weekly window. If your address isn’t eligible, you can still choose shipping. Check your ZIP.
Can I order now?
Yes — both sets are open for pre-order. Pick a size, add to cart, and we’ll ship when they’re ready. You can cancel a pre-order any time before it ships.
When will my pre-order ship?
We show the expected ship timing on each product page and at checkout, and we’ll email you if anything changes.
