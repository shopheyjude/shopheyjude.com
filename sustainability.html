Sustainability

Made to be worn, washed and handed down

We’re a small company, so we keep this simple and honest: here’s what we do today.

100% cotton

Our pajamas are 100% cotton, a natural plant fiber, instead of polyester or other plastic-based fabrics.

Small runs

We make a small number of each print and take pre-orders, so we order closer to what families actually want.

Made to last

Good care keeps pajamas soft for longer, so they can go to a younger sibling or cousin next. Wash cold, dry low, and see the FAQ for care tips.

What we’re still working on

Packaging and shipping materials are next on our list. We’ll share what we choose here, with the facts behind it.
