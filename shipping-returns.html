Shipping & returns

Where we ship

We ship within the United States.

Cost and timing

Shipping costs and delivery times are shown at checkout before you pay.

Local delivery

Local delivery is $7 for eligible addresses in ZIP code 80015. We group deliveries into one weekly window. If your address isn’t eligible, you can still choose shipping.

We confirm each address before delivering and email you once your delivery is scheduled.

Returns & exchanges

Hey Jude is online only, so returns and exchanges happen by mail. There’s no store or drop-off location.

To start a return or swap a size, email hello@shopheyjude.com with your order number and what you’d like to do. We’ll reply with mailing instructions.

Our full return policy will be posted here before pre-orders ship.
