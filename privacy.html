Privacy

Privacy notice

Hey Jude is run by a family, and we keep what we collect to a minimum.

What we collect

  • Email updates: if you sign up for new-pajama emails, your email address — and nothing else.
  • Your account: your email address and order history, so you can sign in and track orders.
  • Orders: checkout is handled by our payment provider, which collects what it needs to process payment and shipping. We receive your order and shipping details, not your full card number.
  • Website hosting: our host may keep standard technical logs (like IP address and browser type) to keep the site secure.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information.
  • We don't knowingly collect information from children. Sign-ups and orders are for adults.
  • We don't use advertising trackers on this site.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email, or contact us to have your information deleted.
