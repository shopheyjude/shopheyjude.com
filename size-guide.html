Size Guide

Finding the right size

Our pajamas are made to fit snugly.

How to measure your favorite PJs

Lay a pair that already fits well on a flat surface. Measure in inches without stretching the fabric.

  1. Chest width: measure straight across from armpit to armpit. This is a flat width across the front, not a measurement around the body.
  2. Top length: measure from the highest point of the shoulder down to the hem.
  3. Relaxed waist: measure across the waistband from one edge to the other, without stretching it. This is also a flat width, not a measurement around the body.
  4. Inseam: measure from the crotch seam down to the hem of the leg.
